Research

Research on supply chain coordination, machine learning for forecasting and bandit pricing, and process improvement.

Research Areas

Supply Chain Coordination

I study how pricing and operational policies can improve coordination and profitability across supply chains, with particular interests in business volume discounts, lot-sizing decisions, and perishable-goods supply chains.

Supply Chain Coordination Business Volume Discounts Perishable Supply Chains

Machine Learning for Forecasting & Bandit Pricing

I apply machine learning to operational decision-making, with current work spanning supervised learning for demand and capacity forecasting and online bandit learning for dynamic pricing and revenue management.

Machine Learning Forecasting Bandit Pricing

Process Improvement Projects

I examine process improvement projects with a focus on Six Sigma, project success, and the organizational and project-level factors that shape improvement outcomes across manufacturing and service settings.

Probabilistic Forecasting of Intermittent Demand Using Natural Gradient Boosting
Omar Al-Zadid, Yuan Wang
Accepted for presentation at the Decision Sciences Institute 2026 Annual Conference; abstract to appear in the conference proceedings.

This study develops a probabilistic machine-learning approach for forecasting intermittent demand by combining Natural Gradient Boosting with a negative binomial distribution. The model improves demand forecasts while explicitly accounting for uncertainty, supporting inventory decisions such as safety stock and reorder-point planning.
