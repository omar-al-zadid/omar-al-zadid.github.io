Research

Research on pricing, supply chains, and data-driven decision making.

My research focuses on how firms can make better operational decisions under uncertainty. I am particularly interested in pricing and discount strategies, supply chain analytics, and quantitative approaches to managerial decision making.

Research Areas

Pricing & Revenue Management

I study how firms can design pricing and discount strategies that improve profitability while accounting for customer behavior and operational constraints.

Pricing Revenue Management Discount Strategy

Supply Chain Analytics

My work examines logistics, distribution, product freshness, uncertainty, and data-driven supply chain decision making.

Supply Chain Logistics Optimization

Decision Analytics

I use analytical modeling, optimization, forecasting, and quantitative methods to study complex managerial problems.
