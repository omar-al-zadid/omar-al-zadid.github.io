Teaching

Helping students understand, recognize, and apply.

My teaching philosophy centers on three goals: building clear and durable understanding, helping students recognize the relevance of operations concepts, and developing the ability to apply those concepts through reasoning and practice.

Teaching Philosophy

Clear Understanding

I organize course material around clear learning goals and reinforce key ideas through repeated engagement, checks for understanding, and structured review.

Recognition of Relevance

I connect concepts to real managerial decisions through cases, practical examples, and applications that help students see when and why a method matters.

Application Through Reasoning

I ask students not only to calculate an answer, but also to explain why a method is appropriate, compare alternatives, and justify their decisions.

Classroom Approach

Practice Before Independence

I model the reasoning process first, then gradually reduce guidance as students become more comfortable solving problems on their own.

Case-Based Learning

Students work with realistic managerial situations so that course concepts are connected to actual decisions rather than isolated formulas.

Applied Analytics

I use hands-on activities and analytical tools to help students move from conceptual understanding to practical application.
